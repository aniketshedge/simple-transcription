# Simple Transcription

Private, local English transcription for audio and video on a server you control.

## For users

- [User Guide](https://github.com/aniketshedge/simple-transcription/wiki/User-Guide) — create jobs, upload recordings, and download results.
- [Processing and Output](https://github.com/aniketshedge/simple-transcription/wiki/Processing-and-Output) — understand stages, progress, warnings, and file formats.
- [Mobile and Connectivity](https://github.com/aniketshedge/simple-transcription/wiki/Mobile-and-Connectivity) — install the PWA and reconnect from a phone.
- [User Troubleshooting](https://github.com/aniketshedge/simple-transcription/wiki/User-Troubleshooting) — find the next step for common problems.

## For self-hosters

- [Self-Hosting](https://github.com/aniketshedge/simple-transcription/wiki/Self-Hosting) — deploy the container on a trusted LAN or Tailscale network.
- [Configuration and Speakers](https://github.com/aniketshedge/simple-transcription/wiki/Configuration-and-Speakers) — tune limits and enable optional speaker labels.
- [Maintenance and Troubleshooting](https://github.com/aniketshedge/simple-transcription/wiki/Maintenance-and-Troubleshooting) — update, monitor, back up, and recover the service.
- [Processing and Output](https://github.com/aniketshedge/simple-transcription/wiki/Processing-and-Output) — the shared explanation of processing and downloads.
